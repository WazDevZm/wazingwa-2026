Put every Zambian business online.

Zambia has hundreds of thousands of small businesses and most of them have no website. Customers search Google, find nothing, and buy elsewhere. Kalulu exists to close that gap — starting with beautiful sites, then growing into the software that runs them.

Named after Kalulu, the clever hare of Zambian folk tales — small, quick, and always outsmarting the bigger animals. That's how we compete with the big agencies.

// FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I own my website?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and you get the full source code on request. No lock‑in.

How do I pay?

Airtel Money, MTN MoMo, Zamtel Kwacha or bank transfer. 50% deposit to start, 50% when you're happy and we launch.

What do I need to give you?

Your logo (or we'll make a simple one), photos, and a short description of what you do. We help write the rest.

Can I update the site myself?

On the Care plan we make edits for you within 48 hours. Self‑editing is coming with Kalulu Sites in 2027.

What happens after the first year?

Hosting renews at K450/month on the Care plan, or you can move the site anywhere you like — it's yours.
